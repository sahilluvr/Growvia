# Growvia roadmap

## ✅ Phase 1 — Email engine, inbox & meetings (v006)
Mailbox connect (SMTP/IMAP) · templates · multi-step campaigns with waits, windows, caps, stop-on-reply · threaded inbox with replies · open/click tracking · unsubscribe + bounces · CSV import, tags, bulk actions, lead timeline · schedule one-off emails · booking page with invites · minute-by-minute scheduler.

## ✅ WhatsApp, Facebook & Instagram (v007)
WhatsApp Business numbers (Cloud API): live chats in Inbox, templates with variables, delivered/read ticks, 24h window, broadcasts to opted-in contacts · Facebook login connects Pages + Instagram · publish photos/videos/carousels/text now or scheduled · Instagram DMs + Messenger in Inbox · signed webhooks.

## Phase 2 — AI content studio
- Real AI writing (Google Gemini free tier; user can add their own OpenAI/Claude key) for plans, emails, posts, replies ("suggest reply" in Inbox).
- Brand kit (logo, colours, fonts, voice) applied everywhere.
- Design studio: branded post images and ad creatives from templates (rendered server-side, free).
- Short promo videos: text + image slideshow reels (9:16) rendered in the browser, exportable as MP4.

## Phase 3 — More social
- LinkedIn (profile/page), YouTube, X, Google Business Profile.
- Content calendar view, first comment, results pulled back (reach, clicks), comment replies.
- Note: publishing for accounts other than your own needs each platform's app review (weeks). Your own + test users work immediately.

## Phase 4 — Lead generation
- Landing page + form builder, website chat widget, pop-ups.
- Local business prospect search (Google Places) for B2B outreach, email verification.
- SMS (Twilio) in the same inbox; WhatsApp click-to-chat ads.
- Analytics: source → lead → customer → revenue.

## Phase 5 — Business
- Billing (Stripe/Razorpay) and plan limits, team seats, agency workspaces, white-label reports.
