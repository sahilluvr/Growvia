If you run a dental office, a law firm, a clinic or a home service company, you've probably sat through a demo that promised to "do all your marketing." Then you discovered the tool mostly does one thing well, like texting or reviews, and charges extra for the rest. Or it does everything, but nobody on your team has time to learn it.

All-in-one marketing software for local service businesses comes in three flavors. Some tools are built for agencies to resell. Some are built for one industry and connect deeply to its practice software. And some are general marketing platforms aimed at owners. Which one fits depends far more on your industry and your existing software than on any feature list.

This guide compares the tools service businesses most often shortlist, explains how each one prices, and gives a simple way to choose by industry.

## What a service business actually needs from marketing software

A local service business has a short, high-stakes customer path. Someone searches, compares a few options on Google Maps, contacts one or two, and books with whoever answers well and fast.

::flow The customer path software has to cover | Found on Google, Maps or an AI assistant | Chooses you from reviews and your website | Calls, texts or fills a form | Gets a fast reply and books | Comes back and refers others

So the jobs are:

1. **Get found:** SEO, Google Business Profile, rankings, and increasingly AI search visibility.
2. **Build trust:** reviews, review replies and a website that converts.
3. **Capture and reply:** forms, chat, texting, calls and a shared inbox.
4. **Follow up and retain:** reminders, recall, nurture emails, rebooking.
5. **Measure:** which channels bring customers, and how you compare with competitors.

No single tool is the best at all five. The real question is which gaps you can live with, and which jobs your existing practice or field service software already handles.

## GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel (often just "HighLevel") is an all-in-one platform built mainly for marketing agencies. It includes a CRM, funnel and website builder, calendars, email and SMS, call features, review requests, and white-labeling so agencies can resell it under their own brand.

**Who it suits:** agencies serving many local clients, and tech-comfortable owners who want one system for funnels, texting and automation.

**Where it falls short:** it's broad, so setup takes real time, and many owners end up buying it through an agency that configures it for them. SEO auditing and rank tracking aren't its core. Texting and calling are usage-billed through its phone system, so costs vary.

**Pricing:** published on [HighLevel's pricing page](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing). Starter is $97 a month, Unlimited $297 and Agency Pro $497, with Enterprise on request. Phone and email usage costs come on top.

## Podium

Podium grew out of review requests by text and now centers on messaging. Website chat turns into a text conversation, and teams answer from one inbox. It adds payments by text, review requests and AI features for answering leads.

**Who it suits:** businesses whose customers prefer texting, such as auto shops, home services and retail with appointments.

**Where it falls short:** it isn't an SEO or content tool, and it won't tell you why a competitor outranks you in Maps. Texting businesses also need to follow the TCPA and 10DLC rules, which we explain in our [SMS marketing guide](/blog/sms-marketing-tcpa-10dlc-guide).

**Pricing:** quote-based. [Podium's pricing page](https://www.podium.com/pricing) asks you to talk to sales.

## Birdeye

Birdeye is a reputation and customer experience platform. It's strongest at review requests, review monitoring across many sites, listings management, surveys and multi-location reporting.

**Who it suits:** multi-location brands and franchises that need review and listing control across dozens of sites. Our [multi-location marketing playbook](/blog/multi-location-marketing-playbook) covers what those brands need beyond reviews.

**Where it falls short:** for a single location on a tight budget, it can be more platform than you need. It isn't built for deep SEO audits or content.

**Pricing:** quote-based and priced by location. [Birdeye's pricing page](https://birdeye.com/pricing/) asks for your number of locations before showing a quote.

## Weave (dental, medical and other offices)

Weave combines phones, patient texting, appointment reminders, reviews and payments, and connects to many practice-management systems. That connection is the point: reminders and recall messages use your real schedule.

**Who it suits:** dental, optometry, veterinary and medical offices that want phones and patient messaging in one system. NexHealth is a common alternative for dental patient communication.

**Where it falls short:** it isn't a full marketing platform. You'll still need help with SEO, AI search visibility, social content and competitor tracking.

**Pricing:** quote-based. Ask for a demo and quote.

A note for healthcare: patient reminders and recall are treatment communications, but using patient information for marketing generally needs written authorization, according to [HHS guidance on HIPAA and marketing](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/marketing/index.html). Keep clinical communication in your HIPAA-compliant patient software. Our [AI marketing for dentists](/blog/ai-marketing-for-dentists) and [AI marketing for medical practices](/blog/ai-marketing-for-medical-practices) guides go deeper.

## Clio Grow (law firms)

Clio Grow is Clio's client intake and legal CRM. It handles intake forms, lead tracking, appointment booking and e-signatures, and passes new clients into Clio's practice management. Lawmatics is the other common choice for legal intake and nurture.

**Who it suits:** firms already on Clio, or any firm whose biggest leak is slow or messy intake. Our guide to [law firm intake and speed to lead](/blog/law-firm-intake-speed-to-lead) explains why that matters.

**Where it falls short:** it isn't an SEO, review or social tool. Firms still need help ranking in Maps and showing up in AI assistants.

**Pricing:** according to [Clio's pricing page](https://www.clio.com/pricing/), Grow is included in higher-tier plans and sold as an add-on to others. Check current pricing with Clio.

## Jobber and ServiceTitan marketing add-ons (home services)

Field service platforms now sell marketing on top of scheduling and invoicing. The advantage is that they know your jobs and customers, so a review request or a maintenance reminder can fire automatically after a job closes.

**Jobber** is popular with small and mid-size home service companies. Its plans cover quoting, scheduling, invoicing and payments. [Jobber's pricing page](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) lists plans from $29 a month on annual prepaid billing (Core) up to $399 (Plus), and a Marketing Suite add-on at $99 a month for review requests, campaigns and referrals.

**ServiceTitan** serves larger residential and commercial contractors with dispatch, call booking, reporting and marketing attribution. Pricing is quote-based, and it suits bigger shops more than a two-truck operation.

**Where they fall short:** neither is built for SEO audits, AI search visibility or tracking how you compare with competitors in Maps. Our [AI marketing for home services](/blog/ai-marketing-for-home-services) pillar shows how these tools fit with call tracking and a marketing platform.

## Questions to ask on every demo

Sales demos show the best path. These questions show the real one.

::chat How to get straight answers on a demo | you: What's the total monthly cost for my team, including seats, texting and phone usage? | them: It depends on volume, but here's a typical bill. | you: What's the contract length, and can I export all my contacts and data if I leave? | them: Annual contract, and yes, CSV export. | you: Which of my current tools does this replace, and what will I still need? | them: You'd keep your practice software and call tracking.

::check Before you sign | Total cost with seats and usage, in writing | Contract length and cancellation terms | Data export in a standard format | Integration with your practice or field service software | Who on your team will own it | A 30-day success measure

Pay attention to compliance too. Tools that send texts must support consent capture and opt-outs under the TCPA. Tools that request reviews must ask every customer, because Google bans review gating and the [FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers) bans buying or suppressing reviews.

::scale Lock-in risk when choosing a platform | Low: month-to-month, full data export, works alongside your existing software | Medium: annual contract, export available, some features tied to their phone numbers | High: multi-year contract, limited export, your website or numbers hosted only by them

## Common mistakes when buying marketing software

- **Buying a second CRM you won't use.** If your practice or field service software already holds customers, pick a marketing tool that complements it.
- **Paying for features nobody owns.** Every tool needs a named person who uses it weekly.
- **Choosing by feature count.** Choose by your biggest leak: being found, slow replies, few reviews or poor follow-up.
- **Ignoring per-seat and usage costs.** These often double the advertised price.
- **Forgetting about email health.** Any tool that sends email needs SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up properly. Our [email deliverability guide](/blog/email-deliverability-spf-dkim-dmarc-guide) explains how.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best all-in-one marketing software for a local service business?

There isn't one best tool for every business. Most service businesses do best with their industry system (practice, legal or field service software) plus one marketing platform that covers SEO, reviews, follow-up and reporting.

### Is GoHighLevel good for small local businesses?

It can be, especially if an agency sets it up for you or you're comfortable with automation tools. It's built mainly for agencies, so expect a learning curve and usage costs for texting and calls on top of the $97 to $497 monthly plans.

### Why don't Podium, Birdeye and Weave publish prices?

They use quote-based pricing that depends on locations, users and features. Ask for the total monthly cost in writing, including onboarding and usage, before you sign.

### Can one tool replace my practice-management or field service software?

Usually not, and you shouldn't try. Keep scheduling, records and clinical or case communication in your industry system, and use marketing software for getting found, reviews, follow-up and reporting.

### Does Growvia send text messages or track calls?

No. Growvia doesn't send SMS or track phone calls. Pair it with a texting tool your industry trusts and with call tracking like CallRail if calls drive most of your leads.

### Is marketing software HIPAA compliant?

It depends on the tool and whether the vendor signs a business associate agreement. Growvia isn't a HIPAA-covered system, so healthcare practices should keep PHI out of it and use HIPAA-compliant patient software for anything clinical.
