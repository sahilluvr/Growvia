If you're a small business in the US (or anywhere) looking at your HubSpot invoice and wondering whether there's a simpler way, this guide is for you. We'll cover why teams switch, what you really use in HubSpot, seven alternatives with honest pros and cons, and how to move without losing a single contact.

## Why small businesses look for HubSpot alternatives

The reasons are rarely about quality. They're about fit and cost.

- **Per-seat billing.** Marketing Hub Starter is priced per seat, roughly $15 to $20 per seat per month depending on billing. Every person who needs access adds to the bill.
- **Contact tiers.** Starter includes 1,000 marketing contacts. A growing list means a growing invoice, even in months you barely send.
- **The Professional jump.** Many teams want better automation, A/B testing, SEO tools or more reporting. In HubSpot those live in Professional, which starts at about $800 a month on annual billing with three seats, plus a required one-time onboarding fee.
- **Complexity.** HubSpot can do almost anything, which means there's a lot to set up, learn and maintain. For a five-person business that's often more platform than needed.

### 2. Zoho CRM and Zoho Campaigns: best low-cost suite

Zoho sells a CRM, email marketing, social, forms and much more as separate apps that work together. Zoho CRM Standard starts around $14 per user per month on annual billing, and Zoho Campaigns has a free plan for up to 2,000 contacts.

**Choose it if:** you want a broad suite at a low price and don't mind setting up several apps.
**Skip it if:** you want one simple workspace. Each Zoho app has its own settings and learning curve.

### 3. Pipedrive: best for sales-led teams

Pipedrive is a sales CRM built around a visual deal pipeline. Sales teams love how quickly they can see what's stuck and what to do next. Marketing features are available as add-ons.

**Choose it if:** your business runs on sales calls and deals, and marketing is secondary.
**Skip it if:** you need SEO, social or WhatsApp. You'll be adding other tools.

### 4. Brevo: best for email and SMS on a budget

Brevo charges by emails sent rather than contacts stored. It has a free plan with up to 300 emails a day and Starter from $9 a month for 5,000 emails, plus SMS and WhatsApp campaigns and a basic CRM.

**Choose it if:** your main need is newsletters and campaigns to a large list.
**Skip it if:** you need SEO or rank tracking.

### 5. ActiveCampaign: best for complex email automation

ActiveCampaign is known for powerful, visual automation builders: if-this-then-that journeys with branching, scoring and goals. It suits businesses with long nurture sequences, like courses and B2B services.

**Choose it if:** automation logic is the heart of your marketing.
**Skip it if:** you want simplicity. Power comes with a learning curve, and SEO and social aren't its focus.

### 6. Mailchimp: best for polished newsletters

Mailchimp has excellent templates and e-commerce integrations. Essentials starts at $13 and Standard at $20 a month for 500 contacts, and pricing rises with your audience size. The free plan is now limited to 250 contacts.

**Choose it if:** newsletters are your main channel and design matters.
**Skip it if:** you want CRM depth or WhatsApp. See our [Mailchimp alternatives guide](/blog/mailchimp-alternatives).

### 7. Freshsales: best for teams that also need support tools

Freshsales, from Freshworks, pairs a sales CRM with built-in phone, email and chat, and connects to Freshdesk for customer support.

**Choose it if:** you want sales and support from one vendor.
**Skip it if:** marketing channels like SEO and social are your priority.

## US-specific things to check before you switch

If you market to customers in the United States, check these before you move:

- **CAN-SPAM.** Every marketing email needs your physical address and a working unsubscribe, and opt-outs must be honoured within 10 business days. Bring your unsubscribe list with you. Our [email laws by country guide](/blog/email-marketing-laws-by-country) explains the details.
- **SMS and the TCPA.** If you text customers, consent rules are strict and A2P 10DLC registration is required. Growvia doesn't send SMS today. Our [SMS marketing guide](/blog/sms-marketing-tcpa-10dlc-guide) covers what to look for in an SMS tool.
- **Local search.** For US local businesses, Google Business Profile and reviews drive a large share of calls. Make sure your new tool helps there. See our [Google Business Profile guide](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-guide) and, for trades, [local SEO for home service businesses](/blog/local-seo-home-service-businesses).

## How to move from HubSpot without losing anything

You don't have to cancel HubSpot on day one. Downgrade instead. The free CRM can stay as an archive while you move your day-to-day work.

::steps Move from HubSpot to Growvia | Export — in HubSpot, export contacts (and companies, if you use them) to CSV, including unsubscribe status | Import — in Growvia, import the CSV into Leads, map the columns and tag each group | Forms — embed your Growvia form where the HubSpot form was, and test a submission | Follow-ups — recreate your top two or three sequences; AI can draft them from a one-line goal | Channels — connect your mailbox, WhatsApp number and Facebook/Instagram | Run both — for two weeks, then downgrade HubSpot to free

A few tips that save headaches:

1. **Export unsubscribes separately** and make sure those contacts aren't emailed from the new tool.
2. **Keep your form field names** the same where you can, so reports stay comparable.
3. **Update your email footer** and booking link everywhere you use them.
4. **Tell your team** which tool is now the source of truth, and when.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a free alternative to HubSpot?

HubSpot's own free CRM is one of the best free CRMs. For free marketing tools in one place, Growvia's free plan includes a website form, a pipeline, 300 emails a month from your mailbox, SEO audits, rank tracking for 10 keywords, the shared inbox and 10 scheduled social posts.

### What is the cheapest HubSpot alternative for a small team?

Growvia Pro costs $19 a month for the whole team, because team members are unlimited on every plan. Zoho is also inexpensive per user. Per-seat tools get more expensive with every person you add.

### Can I import my HubSpot contacts into another tool?

Yes. HubSpot lets you export contacts to CSV, and most alternatives, including Growvia, import CSV files. Export unsubscribes too, so you stay compliant.

### Will I lose my email history if I leave HubSpot?

Old email activity usually stays in HubSpot. That's why downgrading to the free CRM, rather than deleting your account, is a sensible way to keep an archive.

### Is HubSpot worth it for a small business?

It can be, especially if you need a deep CRM, many integrations and detailed reporting, and the budget fits. If you mostly use forms, email and a simple pipeline, a simpler tool will usually cost far less.
