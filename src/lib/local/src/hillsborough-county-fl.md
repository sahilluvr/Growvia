Tampa small business marketing changed in the fall of 2024. Hurricane Helene pushed storm surge into Tampa Bay in late September, and Hurricane Milton made landfall south of the bay about two weeks later, bringing wind and flooding across the region. For months afterward, roofers, restoration crews, tree services, public adjusters and insurance lawyers were flooded with demand, and a lot of homeowners got burned by out-of-town operators who disappeared.

That experience shaped how Hillsborough County customers choose businesses now. They look harder at reviews, licensing, local addresses and how a company behaved after the storms. The businesses that win here are the ones that look local, act honestly and are easy to reach.

Hillsborough County has about 1.5 million people (2020 Census, see [Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/)), with Tampa at its center and fast-growing suburbs like Brandon, Riverview and Apollo Beach. This playbook covers ethical storm-season marketing, the county's growth corridors, its healthcare economy, and specific advice for dentists and law firms.

## Where Hillsborough's customers are

The county mixes an older urban core with some of Florida's fastest suburban growth.

- **South Tampa, Hyde Park and Davis Islands:** older, higher-value homes close to the water. These areas saw heavy surge flooding in 2024, so restoration, elevation, flood-proofing and insurance help are ongoing needs.
- **Downtown, Channelside, Water Street and Ybor City:** condos, nightlife, restaurants and a historic Cuban, Spanish and Italian heritage in Ybor.
- **Seminole Heights, Tampa Heights and Carrollwood:** bungalows and established neighborhoods, lots of renovation work.
- **Westchase, Town 'n' Country and Northdale:** suburban families near the airport and the Veterans Expressway.
- **Brandon, Riverview, Valrico and Apollo Beach (the South Shore):** rapid new-home growth, young families, HOAs and new residents who don't yet have a dentist, plumber or pediatrician.
- **Plant City:** agricultural roots, strawberry country and a smaller-town feel where word of mouth still matters.

MacDill Air Force Base in South Tampa brings military families who move often, so they lean heavily on reviews and recommendations. Spanish is widely spoken across the county, so Spanish service pages help in many categories.

## Storm marketing without being a storm chaser

Tampa Bay residents remember the door-knockers and the trucks with out-of-state plates. If your marketing sounds like theirs, you will lose trust you can't get back.

::compare Storm marketing in Tampa Bay | Storm chaser: urgent "act now" pressure; promises the insurance will pay; no local address; asks for signatures at the door | Trusted local: posts real wait times and service areas; explains the claims process without promises; shows Florida license and local office; follows up in writing

### Before the season

- Publish preparation content in April and May: roof inspections, tree trimming, shutter and generator checks, sump and drainage checks.
- Update your Google Business Profile hours and service areas so they're accurate when you get busy.
- Email past customers a short preparation checklist. Our [HVAC seasonal plan](/blog/hvac-marketing-seasonal-plan) has a calendar you can adapt for any trade.

### After a storm

- Post honest updates on your site and Google Business Profile: what you're handling, how long the wait is, and which areas you can reach.
- Educate, don't promise. Explain how insurance claims and documentation generally work, and tell people to check their own policy.
- Don't pressure people to sign anything on the spot, and follow Florida's contractor rules. Check the current state rules on post-storm contracting and assignment of benefits before you advertise claims help.
- Ask for reviews once the job is done, never in exchange for a discount. The FTC's [consumer reviews rule](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/rule-consumer-reviews-testimonials) bans buying reviews.

## Marketing a law firm in Tampa

Tampa firms are busy with property insurance disputes, personal injury, family law and estate planning, and the storms added a wave of insurance claim questions. For the full firm system, read our [AI marketing guide for lawyers](/blog/ai-marketing-for-lawyers).

### Florida Bar rules in brief

Most TV, radio, print, paid internet ads and unsolicited direct mail or email must be filed with The Florida Bar at least 20 days before first use, at $250 per ad ($750 if late). Firm websites are exempt from filing but still have to follow the content rules. Every ad needs a responsible lawyer's name and a real office location. Florida also bars written solicitation about personal injury or wrongful death within 30 days of the incident. The Bar's [filing requirements page](https://www.floridabar.org/ethics/etad/advertising-filing-requirements/) has the details, and our [Miami-Dade guide](/local-marketing/miami-dade-county-fl) walks through the filing process step by step.

### The law changed, so update your content

Florida made major changes to civil litigation and property insurance law in 2022 and 2023, including changes to attorney fee rules in property insurance cases, the comparative negligence rule and the deadline to file many negligence claims. Pages written before those changes may now be wrong. Have a lawyer review every practice-area page that mentions deadlines, fees or how fault works, and add "check current law" where things may change again.

### What works in Tampa

- **Practice-area pages per city** you truly serve (Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Plant City), with real local details.
- **Post-storm content that educates,** like how to document damage or what an appraisal clause is, without guaranteeing outcomes. Ads based on this content still need filing.
- **Fast intake.** Storm and accident clients call several firms. The first to respond often wins. See our [law firm intake guide](/blog/law-firm-intake-speed-to-lead).
- **Local Services Ads** are available for many legal categories. They are pay-per-lead and need Google's screening. Read our [LSA guide for lawyers](/blog/google-local-services-ads-for-lawyers) and ask your ethics counsel about Florida filing.

This is general information, not legal advice. Our [lawyer advertising rules by state](/blog/lawyer-advertising-rules-by-state) guide compares Florida with other states.

## Marketing a dental practice in Tampa

Hillsborough's growth means a steady supply of new residents looking for a dentist, especially in Riverview, Brandon, Valrico and the rest of the South Shore. Many search "dentist near me" from a new address.

- **Lead with the basics new families want:** insurance plans accepted, new-patient availability, evening or Saturday hours, kids welcome.
- **Location pages done right.** One strong page per real office. Don't create fake pages for towns you don't have an office in. Our [dental SEO guide](/blog/dental-seo-guide) explains how.
- **Military families** from MacDill often use specific insurance plans. If you accept them, say so clearly.
- **Review replies stay generic.** HHS has settled with dental practices for disclosing patient details in Yelp replies, including Elite Dental Associates ($10,000, 2019) and New Vision Dental ($23,000, 2022). See our [dental reviews and HIPAA guide](/blog/dental-office-google-reviews-hipaa).

## Healthcare is a growth engine

Tampa has a large and growing healthcare sector, anchored by Tampa General Hospital, Moffitt Cancer Center, USF Health and BayCare. Around those systems is a big ecosystem of independent practices: physical therapy, chiropractic, dermatology, med spas and specialty clinics.

For independent practices, the main rules are:

- Keep PHI out of marketing tools and review replies.
- Be careful with tracking pixels on booking and portal pages. HHS guidance and a June 2024 court ruling changed part of the picture, but appointment flows remain high risk. Our [healthcare tracking pixels guide](/blog/healthcare-tracking-pixels-privacy) explains.

## Frequently asked questions

### How should Tampa roofers market after a hurricane?

Post honest wait times and service areas, explain the claims process without promising outcomes, show your Florida license and local address, and avoid pressure tactics. Ask for reviews only after the job is complete.

### Do Tampa law firms need to file ads with The Florida Bar?

Most TV, radio, print, paid internet ads and unsolicited direct mail or email must be filed at least 20 days before first use, with a $250 fee per ad. Firm websites are exempt from filing but must follow the content rules.

### What's the best way to reach new residents in Riverview and Brandon?

Make sure you appear in Google Maps for your service in those areas, keep reviews coming, answer common new-resident questions on your site, and check whether AI assistants recommend you for local prompts.

### Can Tampa healthcare practices use Facebook or Google tracking pixels?

Be very cautious. Tracking on booking flows and patient portals remains high risk under HIPAA, so review your setup with your compliance advisor and use privacy-safe analytics.

### Why did my Tampa practice-area pages stop converting after 2023?

Florida changed several civil litigation and insurance rules in 2022 and 2023, so older pages may contain outdated deadlines or fee information. Have a lawyer review and update them.
