Walk into almost any business in San Antonio and you'll hear English and Spanish, often in the same sentence. Bexar County has about 2 million residents (2020 Census, see [Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/)), and a majority of them are Hispanic or Latino. Marketing here works best when it sounds like the city: bilingual by default, warm, and rooted in family and neighborhood.

San Antonio also has two audiences many businesses overlook. Joint Base San Antonio brings a constant flow of military families who arrive, settle in fast and leave a few years later. And the South Texas Medical Center draws patients from across the region.

This guide shows how to market a small business across Bexar County: getting bilingual marketing right, reaching military families, handling the heat, and following Texas rules for lawyers and data privacy.

## Bilingual marketing that actually sounds like San Antonio

Many San Antonians are bilingual, and plenty of Hispanic residents are more comfortable in English than Spanish. Others, including many recent arrivals and older relatives who make family decisions, strongly prefer Spanish. The goal isn't to translate everything word for word. It's to make sure every customer can find you, trust you and talk to you in the language they choose.

::compare Translated vs. truly bilingual marketing | Translated: machine-translated pages; Spanish menu hidden in the footer; English-only phone menu; reviews answered only in English | Bilingual: pages written by fluent speakers; language toggle at the top; "Se habla español" on GBP and the site; reviews answered in the reviewer's language

Practical steps:

- **Write Spanish pages for your core services**, not the whole site. A fluent writer should adapt the wording, not just translate it. "Dentista para niños en San Antonio" and "limpieza dental" are how people actually search.
- **List languages in your Google Business Profile** and name the staff who speak Spanish on your team page. Patients and customers notice.
- **Answer reviews in the language they were written in.** It shows you're listening.
- **Use Spanish in social posts and email when your audience does.** Growvia's AI writing tools can draft posts in Spanish, but have a fluent team member review them before they go out.

::chat Replying to a Spanish-language review | them: Excelente servicio, muy amables y rápidos. ¡Los recomiendo! | you: ¡Muchas gracias por sus palabras! Nos da mucho gusto. Lo esperamos pronto. | them: Gracias a ustedes.

The same privacy rules apply in any language. A healthcare practice must never confirm that a reviewer is a patient, whether the reply is in English or Spanish.

## Military City USA: marketing to JBSA families

Joint Base San Antonio combines Fort Sam Houston, Lackland and Randolph, plus Camp Bullis. It's home to major military training and medical operations, including Brooke Army Medical Center. San Antonio is proud of its "Military City USA" nickname, and many veterans settle here after service.

Military families move often. A family arriving on a permanent change of station (PCS) order has to find a dentist, doctor, mechanic, daycare and housing quickly, usually with little local knowledge.

::steps The PCS cycle and where your business fits | Orders arrive — spouse researches the new area online and in military spouse groups | Arrival — needs housing, utilities, schools and childcare fast | First 90 days — picks a dentist, doctor, vet, mechanic and gym | Settled — leaves reviews and refers other families | Next PCS — needs movers, cleaners, home sellers and lawyers

How to reach them:

- **Say clearly which military insurance and benefits you accept.** For healthcare, that means stating whether you accept TRICARE plans, in plain words on your site and profile.
- **Be findable near the gates.** Families search near Fort Sam Houston, Lackland and Randolph, and in nearby areas like Converse, Universal City, Live Oak and the far west side. Mention the areas you truly serve.
- **Earn word of mouth in spouse networks.** Military spouses share recommendations quickly. Great service and an easy way to leave a review matter more than any ad. See our guide on [getting more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews).
- **Don't imply official endorsement.** Avoid military seals, insignia or wording that suggests the base recommends you.

## Heat is a business season

San Antonio summers are long and hot, and an AC failure in July is an emergency, especially for elderly residents. HVAC and electrical companies see their busiest weeks during heat waves, and the February 2021 winter storm reminded everyone that freezes happen too.

::scale How fast San Antonio customers expect an AC response in summer | Good: same-day response with clear arrival window | Needs work: next-day with no updates | Poor: no answer, no callback, voicemail full

## Marketing a dental practice in San Antonio

San Antonio dental practices compete hardest in growing areas like Stone Oak, Alamo Ranch and near the Medical Center. Three local priorities stand out:

1. **Bilingual front desk and website.** Families often choose a dentist where everyone can communicate comfortably, including grandparents. Say "Se habla español" clearly.
2. **Insurance clarity.** List accepted plans, including whether you accept military dental plans, on your site and in your Google Business Profile.
3. **Safe review replies.** HHS settled with Elite Dental Associates in Dallas for $10,000 in 2019 after the practice disclosed patient information in review replies. ## Marketing a law firm in San Antonio

San Antonio law firms serve a bilingual, family-oriented market. Personal injury, family law, immigration, criminal defense and estate planning are all competitive. Military families create demand for specific work, like family law involving servicemembers and issues under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA), which gives active-duty members certain legal protections.

Key Texas rules:

- **Texas Rule 7.04** requires filing most lawyer advertisements with the State Bar of Texas Advertising Review Committee within 10 days of first use, with a fee, or pre-approval at least 30 days before. Exemptions are in Rule 7.05. Review a Spanish version as carefully as the English one, and ask the committee whether it needs its own filing.
- **The Texas Data Privacy and Security Act** limits how firms use personal data, and sensitive data such as immigration status needs particular care.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a Spanish website to market in San Antonio?

You don't need to translate everything, but Spanish pages for your main services, written by a fluent speaker, help you reach a large share of local customers. Also list Spanish-speaking staff and answer Spanish reviews in Spanish.

### How can I reach military families at Joint Base San Antonio?

State which military insurance and benefits you accept, mention the areas near the bases you serve, and earn reviews from military families who share recommendations in spouse networks. Don't imply official military endorsement.

### When is the busiest season for HVAC companies in San Antonio?

Summer heat waves bring the most urgent calls. Start tune-up campaigns in March and April and make sure every call and form gets a fast response.

### Do San Antonio lawyers need to file ads with the State Bar?

Most ads must be filed with the State Bar of Texas Advertising Review Committee within 10 days of first use under Rule 7.04. If you run Spanish versions, ask the committee whether each one needs its own filing.

### Is San Antonio a good market for AI search visibility?

Yes. Newcomers, including military families, increasingly ask AI assistants for local recommendations. Consistent business details, specific service pages and steady reviews in both languages make you easier to recommend.
