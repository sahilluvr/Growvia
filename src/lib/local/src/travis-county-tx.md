A huge share of Austin's customers didn't grow up here. Travis County has about 1.3 million residents (2020 Census, see [Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/)), and the metro has been one of the fastest-growing in the country for years. People moved in from California, the Northeast and the rest of Texas for jobs in tech, government, healthcare and manufacturing.

For local businesses, that creates an unusual problem. Newcomers have no family dentist, no "guy" for plumbing, and no loyalty to the place that's been on the corner for 30 years. They pick based on what their phone tells them: Google Maps, reviews, and increasingly AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

This guide is about winning those customers in Austin and the rest of Travis County, and keeping them once they've found you.

## Why reviews and AI answers carry extra weight here

In an older, stable market, a business can coast on reputation passed down by word of mouth. In Austin, a large part of the population arrived recently, so that word of mouth hasn't formed yet. Online signals fill the gap.

::flow How an Austin newcomer picks a local business | Asks an AI assistant or searches Maps | Shortlists three to five names | Reads the newest reviews and replies | Checks the website for prices and booking | Books online or sends a form

What this means in practice:

- **Recent reviews matter more than old ones.** A newcomer reads the last few months. A steady flow of reviews beats a burst from three years ago. Our guide to [getting more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews) shows how to ask within Google's rules and the FTC's [rules on reviews and endorsements](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews).
- **AI assistants are a real channel.** Austin's tech-heavy population tends to try new tools early. People ask "best dog groomer in East Austin" or "a dentist near the Domain who's good with anxious patients" and get a short list of names. Consistent business details everywhere, specific service pages and reviews that describe what you do well all help. See our guide on [getting recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity](/blog/get-recommended-by-chatgpt-gemini-perplexity).
- **Prices and booking online.** Many transplants expect to see pricing ranges and book without calling. If you can't show exact prices, explain what affects them.
- **Your competitors are watching the same signals.** Our guide on [why competitors rank above you](/blog/local-competitor-analysis) shows what to compare.

## The Travis County map

Travis County includes Austin plus Pflugerville, Manor, Lakeway, Bee Cave, West Lake Hills, Rollingwood, Sunset Valley, Lago Vista, Jonestown and unincorporated areas like Del Valle. Big suburbs such as Round Rock, Cedar Park, Leander and Georgetown sit mostly in neighboring Williamson County, so if you serve them, note that they're a different county with their own searches.

Inside Austin, neighborhood names carry weight: East Austin, South Congress, Zilker, Barton Hills, Hyde Park, Mueller, Circle C, Riverside and the Domain area in North Austin all show up in searches. Southeast Travis County has grown fast around Tesla's Gigafactory Texas and the Circuit of the Americas, bringing new demand for housing, food, auto and home services.

Austin also has a sizable Spanish-speaking population, especially in East and Southeast Austin, Del Valle and Dove Springs. Fast growth has pushed many long-time families outward, so businesses that serve those communities in Spanish, and respect their history in the neighborhood, tend to earn loyal customers.

Build pages only for areas you truly serve, and include specifics like the roads, landmarks or the type of homes you work on there. Our [local SEO checklist](/blog/local-seo-checklist-small-business) covers the rest.

## Austin's year: events, heat and the occasional freeze

Austin's calendar has a few big moments that change local demand. Plan content and staffing around them.

| When | What happens | Who should plan for it |
|---|---|---|
| January–May of odd-numbered years | Texas Legislature in session at the Capitol | Lawyers, lobbyists, downtown restaurants and hotels |
| March | SXSW brings huge crowds downtown | Restaurants, bars, rideshare-friendly venues, event services |
| May–September | Long, hot summers | HVAC, pool services, electricians, roofers |
| Late August | UT Austin students return | Renters, movers, food, fitness, dental and medical clinics |
| October | Austin City Limits Music Festival in Zilker Park and the Formula 1 race at Circuit of the Americas | Hospitality, short-term rentals, restaurants |

The February 2021 winter storm knocked out power and water across Texas, and the February 2023 ice storm brought down trees and power lines across Austin. Both changed what Austinites search for every winter since: generators, pipe insulation, tree trimming and backup power.

## Wellness, med spas and health data

Austin has a strong wellness culture: med spas, fitness studios, physical therapy, chiropractic and functional medicine all compete for health-conscious newcomers. That brings privacy obligations.

- **HIPAA** applies to covered providers. Never mention treatment in review replies or use patient information in marketing without written authorization.
- **The Texas Data Privacy and Security Act** treats health diagnoses as sensitive data. Small businesses are generally exempt but still can't sell sensitive data without consent. Ad pixels on booking pages deserve a hard look.
- **Biometric check-ins** (face or fingerprint) at gyms and spas raise questions under Texas biometric privacy law. Get advice first.

## Marketing a dental practice in Austin

Austin dental practices compete for a crowd of newcomers who often choose on convenience and reviews. Practices near major employers and the Domain, and in fast-growing areas like Pflugerville and southeast Austin, see heavy competition.

- **Make booking easy.** Online booking or a quick request form, clear hours and an obvious "new patients welcome" message.
- **Explain insurance and pricing.** Newcomers changing jobs often have new plans. Say which plans you accept and what membership options you offer for uninsured patients.
- **Write for the AI shortlist.** Pages that describe specific services, like sedation, Invisalign or emergency visits, help AI assistants match you to detailed questions.
- **Reply to reviews safely.** HHS has settled with dental practices, including Elite Dental Associates in Dallas, over review replies that disclosed patient information. ## Marketing a law firm in Austin

As the state capital, Austin has a legal market shaped by government, regulation and tech. Alongside personal injury, family law and criminal defense, there's steady demand for business formation, startup, employment, real estate and administrative law. Transplants also need Texas-specific help with things like wills and home purchases.

Texas lawyers must follow **Rule 7.04**, which requires filing most advertisements with the State Bar's Advertising Review Committee within 10 days of first use, with a fee, or seeking pre-approval at least 30 days ahead. Exemptions are in Rule 7.05. The Texas Center for Legal Ethics publishes the [current rule text](https://www.legalethicstexas.com/resources/rules/texas-disciplinary-rules-of-professional-conduct/filing-requirements-for-advertisements-and-solicitation-communications/), and our [Harris County page](/local-marketing/harris-county-tx) walks through the filing process and the TDPSA in detail.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why are reviews so important for Austin businesses?

So many residents are recent arrivals that word of mouth hasn't formed yet. Newcomers rely on recent Google reviews, review replies and AI assistant recommendations to choose.

### Do AI assistants really send customers to local businesses in Austin?

Many people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity for local recommendations, and tech-savvy markets like Austin tend to adopt these tools early. Track which questions mention you and which competitors appear instead.

### Are Round Rock and Cedar Park in Travis County?

Mostly no. Round Rock, Cedar Park, Leander and Georgetown are mainly in Williamson County. If you serve them, build separate pages for those cities.

### When should Austin businesses prepare for winter storms?

Publish freeze-prep content in October and keep your profile hours and availability updated during storm weeks. The 2021 winter storm and 2023 ice storm showed how fast demand spikes.

### Do Austin lawyers have to file ads with the State Bar of Texas?

Most lawyer ads must be filed with the Advertising Review Committee within 10 days of first use under Rule 7.04, with exemptions in Rule 7.05. Check the current rules before launching a campaign.
