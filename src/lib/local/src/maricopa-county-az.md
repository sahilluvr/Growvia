Small business marketing in Phoenix runs on two clocks. One is the heat, which turns air conditioning repair into an emergency service from roughly May through September. The other is the snowbird calendar, which fills dental chairs, golf courses, restaurants and real estate showings from about October to April and then empties out. If your marketing plan ignores either clock, you spend money in the wrong months.

Maricopa County is home to about 4.4 million people ([2020 Census](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/)), spread across a valley so wide that "Phoenix" means very different things to a customer in Surprise and a customer in Queen Creek. This guide covers how people in the Valley search, how to compete on Google Maps across a sprawling metro, what changed for Arizona law firms when the state allowed non-lawyer ownership, and a 90-day plan you can start this week.

## The Valley at a glance: one county, many markets

Maricopa County is not one market. It is a cluster of cities that each behave like their own town:

- **Phoenix** itself, from Downtown and Midtown to Arcadia, Ahwatukee and North Phoenix.
- **The East Valley:** Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Tempe (home of Arizona State University) and Queen Creek.
- **The West Valley:** Glendale, Peoria, Surprise, Goodyear, Avondale and Buckeye, where much of the new-home growth has been.
- **Scottsdale and Paradise Valley:** affluent, appearance-focused buyers, and a dense cluster of med spas, cosmetic dentists and boutique fitness studios.
- **Retirement communities** such as Sun City, Sun City West and Sun Lakes, where buying decisions lean on reviews, referrals and trust.

Distance matters here. Freeways make a 25-mile commute feel normal, but a homeowner calling an emergency plumber in August wants someone close, and Google weighs proximity heavily. An office in Tempe will rarely rank in Peoria.

## Who your customers are, and how they search

Three customer groups shape most Valley marketing.

**Year-round residents and transplants.** The metro has grown for decades, and many residents moved from the Midwest or California. Transplants arrive with no loyalty to local businesses, so they lean on Google reviews, "near me" searches and, increasingly, AI assistants. A question like "who's a good family dentist in Gilbert that takes Delta Dental?" is exactly what people now type into ChatGPT or Gemini. Our guide to [getting recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity](/blog/get-recommended-by-chatgpt-gemini-perplexity) explains what those tools look at.

**Seasonal residents (snowbirds).** Winter visitors from colder states and Canada stay for months. They need dentists, physical therapists, salons, restaurants and home watch services. Collect their emails while they are here so you can welcome them back in October.

**Spanish-speaking households.** A large share of Valley residents speak Spanish at home, especially in west and south Phoenix, Maryvale, Avondale and parts of Mesa. Spanish-language service pages, a Spanish-speaking front desk and a Google Business Profile that mentions "Se habla español" in the description (not stuffed into the business name, which Google prohibits) are practical advantages, not extras.

## Heat, monsoon and the Phoenix seasonal calendar

Phoenix summers are dangerous, not just uncomfortable. When an AC unit fails in July, the customer is not comparison shopping for a week. That has real consequences for how you market:

- **HVAC is an emergency trade from May to September.** Answer the phone 24/7 or use an answering service, and keep your Google Business Profile hours accurate. A missed call in July is a job handed to a competitor. Our guide to [missed calls and speed to lead for home services](/blog/missed-calls-speed-to-lead-home-services) covers coverage options.
- **Monsoon season** officially runs June 15 to September 30, according to the [National Weather Service in Phoenix](https://www.weather.gov/psr/). Dust storms, microbursts and flash flooding damage roofs, fences, pools and trees. Roofers and restoration companies should publish storm-response pages before the season, not after the first storm. Our [roofing marketing and storm season guide](/blog/roofing-marketing-storm-season) explains how to do it without storm-chaser tactics.
- **Fall and winter** bring snowbirds, golf, patio dining and peak real estate activity.
- **Spring** is the time to sell AC tune-ups, pool service contracts and pest control before heat sets in.

## Marketing a dental practice in Phoenix

Phoenix dental marketing splits into two worlds. In Scottsdale, Paradise Valley and Arcadia, cosmetic work, veneers, implants and clear aligners dominate, and patients compare before-and-after galleries and reviews closely. In the West Valley and the newer suburbs, families want a nearby general dentist who takes their insurance and has Saturday or early-morning hours.

A few Valley-specific moves:

1. **Build one strong page per location you actually serve from.** If you have an office in Chandler, rank in Chandler. Don't create thin "dentist in Buckeye" pages for a city 40 miles away.
2. **Market to snowbirds honestly.** A "seasonal patients welcome" page that explains how you coordinate records with their home dentist is useful and earns trust. Time recall and reactivation emails for October.
3. **Offer Spanish-language pages** for core treatments if your team can serve Spanish-speaking patients in person.
4. **Keep review replies HIPAA-safe.** Never confirm someone is a patient in a reply. HHS OCR has settled with practices over exactly this.

## Marketing a law firm in Phoenix

Arizona's legal market is unusual. Since January 1, 2021, Arizona has allowed non-lawyers to own or share fees in law firms through **Alternative Business Structures (ABS)**, after the Arizona Supreme Court eliminated Ethics Rule 5.4. ABS entities are licensed by the Arizona Supreme Court and must designate a compliance lawyer, among other requirements. The court publishes details and an annual report from its [Committee on Alternative Business Structures](https://www.azcourts.gov/cld/Alternative-Business-Structure).

What that means for marketing: some of your competitors may be backed by outside investors or run by business owners who are not lawyers, and they may outspend a solo practice on ads and intake staff. A small firm wins by being faster, more specific and more trusted, not by matching budgets.

Arizona also changed its advertising rules in 2021. According to the [State Bar of Arizona](https://www.azbar.org/for-legal-professionals/ethics/best-practices/er-7-1-communications-concerning-a-lawyer-s-services-and-er-7-3-solicitation-of-new-clients), Arizona lawyers may now say they "specialize" in an area where they have genuine expertise or limit their practice to it, and the restrictions on paying for referrals were loosened. Communications must still name the responsible lawyer or firm and include contact information, must not be false or misleading, and live person-to-person solicitation is still restricted. Unlike Texas, Florida and Nevada, Arizona does not run a mandatory pre-filing program for lawyer ads as of this writing. Check the State Bar's current guidance before launching a campaign; this is not legal advice.

Practical steps for a Phoenix firm:

- **Answer within minutes.** Personal injury, DUI and family law clients call several firms. Our [law firm intake guide](/blog/law-firm-intake-speed-to-lead) has scripts and follow-up cadences.
- **Track AI prompts.** "Best DUI lawyer in Phoenix" is a real kind of query people ask assistants. The [law firm AI search visibility guide](/blog/law-firm-ai-search-visibility) shows how to track and improve it.
- **Market in Spanish** for practice areas like personal injury, immigration and family law, with Spanish-speaking intake staff.

The [AI marketing for lawyers pillar](/blog/ai-marketing-for-lawyers) covers the full law firm growth system, and our [lawyer advertising rules by state](/blog/lawyer-advertising-rules-by-state) guide compares Arizona with other states.

> **Key takeaway for Valley law firms:** ABS competition means some rivals have deep pockets. Your edge is speed to answer, a narrow practice focus you can credibly describe, and reviews that mention real outcomes without breaching client confidentiality.

## Arizona rules that affect your marketing

| Topic | What applies in Arizona | Who should care |
|---|---|---|
| Law firm ownership | Non-lawyer ownership allowed via licensed ABS since 2021 | Law firms, legal investors |
| Lawyer advertising | ER 7.1–7.3 as amended; "specialize" allowed with genuine expertise; no Texas-style filing program | All Arizona lawyers |
| Comprehensive privacy law | No comprehensive consumer privacy law like California's as of this writing | Most small businesses (still follow FTC rules) |
| Health information | HIPAA applies to covered providers; keep PHI out of marketing tools | Dentists, clinics, med spas, PT |
| Reviews | FTC Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR Part 465) | Every business |
| Texting | TCPA consent and 10DLC registration | Anyone sending SMS |
| Contractor licensing | Arizona Registrar of Contractors licenses most contractors; show your license number | Roofers, HVAC, plumbers, electricians |

You can verify contractor licenses at the [Arizona Registrar of Contractors](https://roc.az.gov/). Displaying your ROC number on your site and profile is an easy trust signal for homeowners who have been warned about unlicensed work.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the busiest season for Phoenix businesses?
It depends on your trade. HVAC, pool and pest companies peak from May through September, while dentists, restaurants, golf, salons and real estate often peak from October through April when seasonal residents are in town.

### Do Arizona lawyers have to file their ads with the State Bar?
Arizona does not run a mandatory ad-filing program like Texas or Florida as of this writing, but ads must still follow ER 7.1–7.3. Check the State Bar of Arizona's current guidance before you launch, and treat this as general information, not legal advice.

### What are Alternative Business Structures in Arizona?
They are law firms licensed by the Arizona Supreme Court that may include non-lawyer owners or fee sharing, allowed since January 1, 2021. For marketing, it means some competitors may have outside investment and larger ad budgets.

### Should I market in Spanish in Maricopa County?
If your team can serve Spanish-speaking customers in person or by phone, yes. Build real Spanish-language service pages and mention Spanish service in your profile description, not in your business name.

### Can one office rank on Google Maps across the whole Valley?
Usually not. Proximity is a major local ranking factor, so focus on the cities near your office and open a real location before trying to rank in a distant part of the metro.
