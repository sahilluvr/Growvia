## Three King Counties: Seattle, the Eastside and South King

Treat the county as at least three markets.

- **Seattle.** Neighborhood identity is strong: Capitol Hill, Ballard, Fremont, Queen Anne, West Seattle, Columbia City, the University District. People expect a business to be close, walkable or a short drive across a bridge they don't hate.
- **The Eastside.** Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Issaquah and Sammamish, home to major tech employers including Microsoft in Redmond. Households tend to be busy, high-income and comfortable paying for convenience and quality. Many residents relocated for work, often from other countries.
- **South King County.** Renton, Kent, Auburn, Federal Way, Tukwila and Burien are among the most diverse areas in the state, with families who speak Spanish, Vietnamese, Somali, Amharic, Tagalog, Korean, Chinese and many other languages at home.

Lake Washington and its bridges split Seattle from the Eastside, and traffic on I-5, I-405 and SR 520 makes a 10-mile trip feel long. For Google Maps, that means rank where your customers actually are. A Ballard dental office should not expect to rank in Bellevue.

## Washington's My Health My Data Act, in plain English

The My Health My Data Act (MHMDA) is Washington's consumer health data law. It covers health-related data that HIPAA does not, and it reaches many businesses that don't think of themselves as health care: med spas, gyms, wellness studios, nutrition coaches, supplement shops, fertility and period apps, and the marketing side of dental and medical practices.

::stats MHMDA key facts | Mar 31, 2024 | main obligations took effect | Jun 30, 2024 | date for small businesses | 2,000 ft | geofencing ban radius around health care facilities

According to the [Washington Attorney General's MHMDA page](https://www.atg.wa.gov/protecting-washingtonians-personal-health-data-and-privacy), the law requires businesses that collect consumer health data to:

- **Publish a consumer health data privacy policy** and link to it prominently on the homepage.
- **Get consent before collecting** consumer health data, beyond what's needed to provide the service the person asked for, and **separate consent before sharing** it.
- **Get a signed authorization before selling** it, and keep those authorizations for six years.
- **Honor requests** to access and delete the data.

The law also bans using a geofence within 2,000 feet of an in-person health care facility to identify or track consumers, collect their health data, or send them health-related ads. And violations are treated as violations of Washington's Consumer Protection Act, which means consumers can sue, not just the Attorney General. That private right of action is why lawyers watch this law so closely.

### What MHMDA means for your marketing

- **Audit your website tracking.** A Meta Pixel or other tag that sends "booked a Botox consult" or "viewed our weight-loss page" to an ad platform may involve consumer health data. Our [healthcare tracking pixels guide](/blog/healthcare-tracking-pixels-privacy) explains safer setups.
- **Check your forms.** If an intake or contact form asks about symptoms, treatments or conditions, you need consent language and a privacy policy that covers it.
- **Don't geofence clinics or hospitals** for ads, including competitors' locations.
- **Keep health details out of general marketing tools.** Growvia is not a HIPAA-covered system, and the same caution applies under MHMDA. Use it for marketing to people who opted in, and keep treatment and health details in your clinical or booking software.

> **Not legal advice:** MHMDA's definitions are broad and the private right of action raises the stakes. Have a Washington privacy attorney review your website, forms and ad setup, especially if you run a med spa, wellness business or clinic.

## Marketing a dental practice in Seattle

Seattle-area patients are detail-oriented, insurance-aware and quick to read reviews. A few moves that work here:

1. **Separate HIPAA data from marketing data.** Patient records and treatment details belong in your practice management and HIPAA-compliant communication tools. Marketing data from your website may fall under MHMDA. Treat both carefully.
2. **Publish a clear insurance page.** Many large employers offer dental benefits, so "do you take my plan?" is the first question patients ask.
3. **Serve multilingual families** in South King County and the Eastside if your team can. A page in the language your staff speaks, plus a note in your profile description, helps families find you.
4. **Reply to reviews without confirming anyone is a patient.** HHS OCR has settled with dental practices that disclosed patient information in review replies. See [dental reviews and HIPAA](/blog/dental-office-google-reviews-hipaa).

## Marketing a law firm in Seattle

Washington simplified its lawyer marketing rules in January 2021. According to the [Washington State Bar Association](https://nwsidebar.wsba.org/2021/01/19/washington-supreme-court-approves-major-changes-to-lawyer-marketing-rules/), the Washington Supreme Court eliminated former RPCs 7.2, 7.4 and 7.5 and moved their content into the comments to RPC 7.1, which centers on not making false or misleading communications. Lawyers may describe themselves as "specialists" if the claim is accurate, and RPC 7.3 on solicitation was narrowed to its core limits (no misleading statements, no contact with someone who can't exercise judgment, and no harassment or contact after a person says no). Washington does not have a Texas- or Florida-style ad filing program as far as we know. Check the current rules with the WSBA; this is general information, not legal advice.

For Seattle firms, that flexibility means the constraint is less about rules and more about trust:

- **Be specific.** "Seattle tenant-side landlord-tenant attorney" or "Bellevue H-1B and green card lawyer" beats "full-service law firm."
- **Show up in AI answers.** Tech-savvy clients ask assistants for lawyer recommendations. The [law firm AI search visibility guide](/blog/law-firm-ai-search-visibility) explains how to track and improve it.
- **Answer fast.** The [law firm intake guide](/blog/law-firm-intake-speed-to-lead) covers response times and follow-up.

## Rain, wind and the occasional heat wave

The Seattle area's wet season runs roughly from October through April, and it drives steady work for roofers, gutter cleaners, moss removal companies and drainage contractors. Two recent events also changed what homeowners search for:

- **The June 2021 Pacific Northwest heat wave** pushed Seattle to an all-time record of 108 degrees, according to the [National Weather Service in Seattle](https://www.weather.gov/sew/). Many homes had no air conditioning, and interest in heat pumps and AC installs has stayed higher since.
- **The November 2024 "bomb cyclone"** brought damaging winds and widespread power outages, which raised interest in tree work, roof repair and backup generators.

::compare Seasonal marketing for a King County home services company | Fall and winter: gutter cleaning and moss removal campaigns; roof inspection before storms; storm repair pages ready; generator and backup power content | Spring and summer: heat pump and AC install content in May; exterior painting and deck work; pressure washing; review requests after every job

Contractors in Washington register with the [Department of Labor & Industries](https://lni.wa.gov/licensing-permits/contractors/), and homeowners can look up registration status. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does Washington's My Health My Data Act apply to my small business?
It can apply to any business that collects consumer health data in Washington that HIPAA doesn't cover, including med spas, gyms and wellness businesses. Small businesses had to comply by June 30, 2024. Ask a Washington privacy attorney about your setup.

### Can I run geofenced ads near hospitals in Seattle?
No. MHMDA bans geofencing within 2,000 feet of an in-person health care facility to track consumers, collect health data or send health-related ads.

### Do Washington lawyers have to file ads with the bar?
Washington does not run a Texas- or Florida-style filing program as far as we know, and its 2021 rule changes simplified lawyer marketing. Ads must still be truthful under RPC 7.1, so check current WSBA guidance.

### Are AI assistants really important for Seattle businesses?
For many categories, yes. Seattle has a lot of tech-savvy buyers who ask assistants for recommendations, so consistent business details, specific reviews and clear service pages matter more here.

### When should Seattle roofers and gutter companies market?
Start in late summer and early fall, before the wet season, then keep storm repair pages ready from October through April.
