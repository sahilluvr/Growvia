Fort Lauderdale small business marketing runs on a calendar most of the country doesn't have. Snowbirds arrive in the fall and leave in spring. Hurricane season overlaps the slow months. Condo associations are working through repair projects that didn't exist on this scale a few years ago. If your marketing runs the same message all year, you are leaving money on the table.

Broward County has about 1.9 million people (2020 Census, see [Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/)) spread across more than 30 cities, from Fort Lauderdale and Hollywood on the coast to Pembroke Pines, Miramar and Weston out west. The customer in a Galt Ocean Mile condo and the customer in a Coral Springs subdivision want different things, search differently and respond to different offers.

This guide covers how to plan around Broward's seasons, how to win the Maps pack in your part of the county, and what dentists, law firms, contractors and marine businesses should do differently here.

## Know which Broward you're selling to

Broward splits roughly into three markets, and most businesses only serve one or two of them well.

- **The coast (Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pompano Beach, Deerfield Beach, Lauderdale-by-the-Sea):** condo towers, retirees, seasonal residents, tourists, boaters, and the Las Olas dining and nightlife scene.
- **Central Broward (Plantation, Sunrise, Lauderhill, Lauderdale Lakes, Tamarac, Davie):** established neighborhoods, large Caribbean communities, busy commercial corridors along Broward Boulevard, Sunrise Boulevard and University Drive.
- **West and southwest (Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Weston, Coral Springs, Parkland):** younger families, newer homes, HOAs, school-driven decisions.

Language matters too. Spanish is widely spoken across the county. Haitian Creole and Jamaican and other Caribbean communities are a big presence in central Broward, and there is a sizable Brazilian community in the Pompano and Deerfield area, so Portuguese can open a door competitors ignore.

## Plan around snowbird season and storm season

Seasonal residents typically arrive in the fall and stay through spring. That shifts demand in ways you can plan for.

| Period | What changes in Broward | What to market |
|---|---|---|
| October–December | Seasonal residents return, condos reopen | AC tune-ups, plumbing checks after months empty, dental and medical appointments, home watch services |
| January–April | Peak population, peak tourism and boating | Restaurants, salons, med spas, marine services, real estate showings |
| May–June | Seasonal residents leave, hurricane prep begins | Home watch, shutters, generator service, roof inspections |
| June 1–November 30 | Atlantic hurricane season ([NHC](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/)) | Storm prep content, honest post-storm response, flood and water damage restoration |

Flooding is not only a hurricane problem here. In April 2023, Fort Lauderdale got more than two feet of rain in roughly a day, flooding homes and closing the airport. Restoration, plumbing and roofing companies that already had clear "water damage" pages and fast response paths were easy to find when people searched in a panic.

> A simple seasonal rule for Broward: whatever your customers will need in three months, start publishing about it now. Google needs time to rank new pages, and seasonal residents often book before they fly down.

## Contractors and the condo repair wave

After the 2021 collapse of Champlain Towers South in nearby Surfside, Florida changed its condo safety laws. The new rules added required structural inspections for many older condo buildings and stricter reserve requirements for major repairs, and the legislature has adjusted the details since. Check the current statutes before you cite specifics in your marketing.

For Broward contractors, engineers and property managers, that means a steady stream of associations looking for inspections, concrete restoration, waterproofing, roofing, windows and project management. These are board decisions, not impulse buys.

How to market to condo boards:

1. **Build a page for each service boards buy,** such as concrete restoration, balcony repair or roof replacement, with the cities you serve.
2. **Publish plain-English explainers** on what a board should ask a contractor, how a project is phased, and how residents are kept informed. Don't give legal advice about the statute.
3. **Show licensing and insurance** clearly. Boards and managers check.
4. **Collect reviews from board members and property managers,** not only individual owners.
5. **Use email follow-up.** Board decisions take months. A short sequence keeps you in front of them between meetings.

Do you work in Pompano Beach? | you: Yes, we do concrete restoration in Pompano and across Broward. Can you share the engineer's report so we can scope it? | them: Sure. The board meets on the 15th. | you: We'll send a written proposal and a phasing plan by the 10th, so the board has time to review it.

## Boaters, marinas and marine services

Fort Lauderdale's canals, marinas and Port Everglades support a big marine economy: boat detailing, mobile mechanics, canvas and upholstery, yacht management, charters and dockside services. Marine customers often search by marina or neighborhood ("boat detailing Las Olas Isles"), and many are seasonal or out of state.

- Make your Google Business Profile service area match the marinas and waterways you actually serve.
- Ask customers to mention the boat type and location in reviews.
- Send a pre-season email before seasonal owners return, and a hurricane plan reminder in May.

## Marketing a dental practice in Fort Lauderdale

Broward dental patients split into two groups: year-round families in the western suburbs, and older or seasonal patients on the coast who care about implants, dentures and convenient scheduling around their travel.

## Marketing a law firm in Fort Lauderdale

Broward is home to a large legal community around the county courthouse downtown, and firms compete hard for personal injury, family, elder law, estate planning and condo or HOA disputes. The retiree and seasonal population makes elder law and estate planning especially active. For the full growth system, read our [AI marketing guide for lawyers](/blog/ai-marketing-for-lawyers).

Florida Bar rules apply in full. The short version: most TV, radio, print, paid internet ads and unsolicited direct mail or email must be filed with The Florida Bar at least 20 days before first use, with a $250 fee per ad ($750 if late), while firm websites are exempt from filing. See The Florida Bar's [filing requirements](https://www.floridabar.org/ethics/etad/advertising-filing-requirements/). We go through the filing process, exemptions, "advertisement" labels and the 30-day rule on accident solicitations in our [Miami-Dade guide](/local-marketing/miami-dade-county-fl), and compare states in [lawyer advertising rules by state](/blog/lawyer-advertising-rules-by-state).

Practical tips for Broward firms:

- **Estate planning and elder law:** seminars at clubhouses and community centers, followed by an email nurture sequence. Our [family law and estate planning marketing guide](/blog/family-law-estate-planning-marketing) covers long decision cycles.
- **Condo and HOA law:** the condo repair wave also creates disputes over assessments and contracts. Plain-English articles for owners and boards can rank well, but avoid anything that reads like a guarantee.
- **Reviews:** ask at the end of a matter, and never reveal client details in replies. Our [lawyer reviews ethics guide](/blog/lawyer-reviews-ethics-google) explains ABA Formal Opinion 496.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check The Florida Bar's current rules.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should Fort Lauderdale businesses run seasonal promotions?

Start promoting fall services in September and October, before seasonal residents return, and run peak-season offers from January through April. Hurricane prep content works best in May and early June.

### How can contractors market condo inspection and repair work in Broward?

Build service pages for what condo boards buy, publish plain-English explainers for boards, show licensing and insurance clearly, and follow up by email through the board's decision cycle. Don't give legal advice about the condo statutes.

### Do Broward law firms have to file ads with The Florida Bar?

Most TV, radio, print, paid internet ads and unsolicited direct mail or email must be filed at least 20 days before use, at $250 per ad. Firm websites are exempt from filing but must still follow the content rules.

### Should Broward businesses market in languages other than English?

Often, yes. Spanish is widely spoken across the county, and Haitian Creole and Portuguese can reach communities that most competitors ignore. Only promote a language if someone on your team can actually serve customers in it.
